# Privacy Policy for "亮了沒？"

Effective date: November 16, 2026

"亮了沒？" ("the App") is an educational game about electric circuits. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- No account is required, and the App never asks for personal information.
- The App does not connect to the internet. No data leaves your device.
- The App contains no ads and no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

The App stores your game progress (completed levels, lessons viewed, play time) and your settings (music, sound effects, haptics) on your device only. We cannot access this data. You can erase it at any time by choosing "重置進度" (Reset Progress) in the App's settings, or by deleting the App.

The App is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no personal data from children.

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date.
